PDFSpacer · Free online PDF tool

Make room for your answers.

Add blank space between questions in a PDF worksheet, exam paper or set of notes. Click where you need room to write, and PDFSpacer moves the content below down.

No signup or watermark. Your PDF stays on your device.

How to add blank space to a PDF

  1. Choose your PDF. Open a worksheet, past paper or document from your device.
  2. Insert space. Select the ruler tool, then click in the gap below a question. Drag the spacer’s handle to make more room.
  3. Export your PDF. Choose Paginated A4 for printing or Single Long Page for scrolling.

Writing space that fits the question

Leave a plain area for diagrams, add ruled lines for written answers, or use squared and dot grids for calculations. Insert several spaces on a page and resize each one.

For example, put room for your working between question 1 and question 2. The second question moves down, and longer documents continue onto new pages when exported as A4.

Questions about PDF spacing

Does adding space cover the original text?

The spacer pushes the content below it down. Place it in a clear gap between lines or questions so the insertion point does not cut through text or a diagram.

Can I add space to a scanned PDF?

Yes. You can insert space between parts of a scanned page. PDFSpacer does not recognize or edit the words in the scan; choose the insertion point yourself.

Can I type my answers here?

PDFSpacer creates the writing space. Export the PDF, then print it to write by hand or open it in your preferred PDF annotation app.

Is this PDF tool free and private?

Yes. No account, payment or watermark is required. PDF processing happens in your browser. The app saves your session on this device; use Project → Clear Session to remove the saved PDF and layout.
