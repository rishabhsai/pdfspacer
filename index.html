How to add blank space between PDF questions
  1. Open your PDF. Choose a worksheet, exam paper or set of notes.
  2. Add space. Click in a clear gap below a question. Choose plain, ruled, squared or dotted space and adjust its height.
  3. Export. Preview the result, then download A4 pages for printing or a single long page for digital writing.

PDFSpacer shifts the content below each insertion point down. Scanned pages work too; place the space between lines to avoid cutting through text or diagrams.
