How to add blank space to a PDF

  1. Open your PDF. Choose a worksheet, exam paper, homework sheet or set of notes. Scanned PDFs work too.
  2. Click where you need room. Click in the gap below a question. Pick plain, ruled, squared or dot-grid paper and drag the handle to set the height in millimetres.
  3. Export. Download printable A4 pages or a single long page for writing on a tablet. Text and graphics stay sharp; nothing is rasterised.

Common questions

How do I add blank space between questions in a PDF?

Open the PDF in PDFSpacer, click in the gap below a question, choose a paper style and height, then export. Everything below the insertion point moves down to make room.

Is PDFSpacer free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no page limit. It runs entirely in your browser.

Does my PDF get uploaded?

No. The file is opened and processed on your device. Only the PDF rendering libraries are loaded from a CDN.

Can I add space to a scanned PDF?

Yes. A scanned page is split and spaced in the same way. Click in a clear gap so the split does not cut through a line of text.

Can I print the result on A4?

Yes. The Paginated A4 export flows the expanded document across standard A4 pages. Turn on A4 breaks in the editor to preview where pages will split.

How is this different from adding margins or blank pages?

Margin tools add room around the edge of a page. Blank-page tools insert a whole empty sheet. PDFSpacer inserts space inside the page at the point you choose, directly under the question.
