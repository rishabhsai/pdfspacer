A few useful answers.

Before you make room.

How do I add space between questions in a PDF?

Choose your PDF, select Add space, then click in the gap below a question. Adjust the height or drag the bottom handle. The following content moves down. Preview your result before exporting.

Will a spacer cover my existing text?

The spacer moves the content below it down. Put the insertion point in a clear gap so it does not cut through a line of text or a diagram. Undo is available if you choose the wrong spot.

Can I add blank space to a scanned PDF?

Yes. You choose where to split the scanned page and insert writing space. PDFSpacer does not recognize or change the words in a scan.

Can I type answers or edit the words in my PDF?

PDFSpacer creates room for writing; it does not edit the original words. Download your PDF to print it or write on it with your preferred annotation app. Interactive forms, annotations and links are not carried over into the spaced document.

Is it really free? Where does my PDF go?

There is no account, payment or watermark. Processing happens in your browser. Your PDF and layout are saved on this device so you can return later. Use Project → Clear session to remove that saved copy.

Can I print the PDF or use it on a tablet?

Choose Paginated A4 for printing or Single long page for scrolling. Both preserve the source text and graphics. A scanned page retains the quality of the original scan. Use the export preview to check the result.
