Add lines to a PDF worksheet for students to write answers

A worksheet with questions and no lines invites messy answers. Drawing lines one at a time with a PDF annotation tool is slow, and converting the PDF to Word usually breaks the layout. PDFSpacer takes a different approach: click below a question and it inserts a block of ruled lines, moving the next question down to make room.

The result is a normal PDF you can print or share, and the original questions are untouched.

How to add answer lines to a worksheet

  1. Open the worksheet in PDFSpacer. It can be an exported Word document, a scanned sheet or a PDF from a textbook publisher.
  2. Click in the gap under a question. A spacer appears. In the panel on the right, set Paper style to Ruled.
  3. Set the height and line spacing. The height decides how many lines appear. Pattern spacing sets the gap between lines.
  4. Duplicate for the other questions. Select the spacer, click Duplicate, and drag the copy under the next question so every answer area matches.
  5. Export. Choose Paginated A4 for printing and download the PDF.

Choosing a line spacing

  • Early years and Key Stage 1: 12 to 15 mm lines give room for large handwriting.
  • Upper primary: 10 mm.
  • Secondary and adult learners: 8 mm, the same as a standard exercise book.

The lines are light grey so they sit behind handwriting and do not compete with the printed question. For maths worksheets choose Squared instead, and for drawing tasks choose Plain or Dot grid.

Lines, boxes or blank space?

Ruled lines suit sentence and paragraph answers. For a single-word or numeric answer a short plain spacer of 15 to 20 mm is enough and looks cleaner than a lined block. For working out, squared paper helps pupils keep columns aligned. You can mix styles on the same page; each spacer has its own style.

Worksheets for Google Classroom and tablets

Export as Single Long Page if pupils will complete the sheet on a tablet. One tall page scrolls smoothly in Kami, GoodNotes, Notability and the Google Classroom annotation view, and there are no page breaks to land in the middle of an answer. Use Paginated A4 when the class will work on paper.

The exported PDF keeps the original text as text. Interactive form fields, links and existing annotations are not included, so add lines before you add any fillable fields in another tool.

Common questions

Can I add lines on top of the existing text?

No. PDFSpacer inserts new space rather than drawing over the page, so a spacer always pushes the content below it down. That is what keeps the printed questions readable.

Can I change the colour or thickness of the lines?

Not at the moment. Lines are printed in a light grey at a fixed thickness, which prints reliably on any printer and photocopier.

My worksheet is a Word document. Can I use it?

Export it to PDF first from Word or Google Docs, then open the PDF in PDFSpacer.

Does it work on a scanned worksheet?

Yes. Click in a gap between questions and the scan is split there. Very tight gaps can be widened by zooming in before you click.
