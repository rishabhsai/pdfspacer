Add space to a PDF for notes

To add room for notes to a PDF of lecture slides, an essay or a reading, open it in PDFSpacer and click under the slide or paragraph where you want to write. A gap of plain, ruled, squared or dot-grid paper is inserted there and everything below moves down. One click on Every page instead adds a whole notes page after every page. Export, and the result opens in GoodNotes, Notability or any PDF reader.

It works in the browser, is free, needs no account, and the PDF never leaves your device.

How to add note space to a PDF

  1. Open the PDF in PDFSpacer. Slide decks exported from PowerPoint or Keynote, journal articles and scanned textbook chapters all work.
  2. Click where you want to write. Click in a gap below a slide, heading or paragraph. A spacer appears and the rest of the page moves down.
  3. Pick a paper style. Plain for free-form notes, Ruled for text, Dot grid for diagrams and mind maps, Squared for equations.
  4. Repeat after each section you want to annotate, then click Export PDF.

Notes on an iPad or Android tablet

Export as Single Long Page. The whole document becomes one continuous sheet, which is the easiest format to handwrite on in GoodNotes, Notability, Samsung Notes, Xodo or Apple's Preview. There are no page breaks, so a note never gets cut in half. Import the exported file the same way you would any other PDF.

If you prefer separate pages, or you plan to print, choose Paginated A4. Turn on A4 breaks in the editor to see where each page will end.

A layout that works for lecture slides

For a deck exported one slide per page, click Every page, choose Fill the rest of the sheet and pick ruled lines: each slide sits at the top of an A4 sheet with lines to the bottom. The slides guide walks through it. For decks exported at several slides per page, insert a plain spacer of 60 to 90 mm below each slide instead. Use Duplicate to copy the spacer so every slide gets the same amount of room, and adjust individual ones later where a topic needs more.

Add pages or space to a PDF of your essay or lecture notes

If you are trying to add pages to a PDF version of an essay or your lecture notes so there is somewhere to write, you usually do not need whole blank pages. Click under the paragraph or slide you want to comment on and the room opens exactly there, with the rest of the document pushed down. If you do want a full blank page after every page, use Every page in the toolbar; it adds one in a single click.

This also works for a reading you have been set: insert a short ruled spacer after each section, print it or open it on a tablet, and your summary sits next to the text it summarises.

How to add space in Notability or GoodNotes

Notability and GoodNotes can insert a blank page after the current one, but neither can add one after every page at once or push the rest of a page down to open a gap under a paragraph. Run the PDF through PDFSpacer first, then import the exported file. The space is part of the PDF itself, so it stays in place on every device and in any app, and you can still use the app's own blank pages on top.

Space below the content or in the margin?

PDFSpacer adds space between parts of a page, across its full width. It does not widen the side margins. If you want a blank column beside the original page for margin notes, a margin tool is the better fit. If you want notes that follow the flow of the document, with each note under the slide, question or paragraph it relates to, that is what PDFSpacer is for. The two approaches can be combined by running the margin tool first.

The exported PDF keeps text as text, so you can still search and highlight it. Links, form fields and existing annotations are not carried across, so add your own annotations after exporting.

Common questions

How can I add pages to a PDF of my essay or lecture notes?

Open the PDF in PDFSpacer, click Every page and keep Full blank page selected. A blank, ruled, squared or dot-grid page is added after every existing page, so each slide or essay page is followed by a page for your notes. For a notes page after only some pages, use Add space and click at the bottom of those pages instead.

How do I make space on a PDF to write notes on an iPad?

Add the space in PDFSpacer first, export as Paginated A4 or Single long page, then open the file in GoodNotes, Notability or Apple Notes. The gaps are part of the PDF, so you write directly under the slide with the Apple Pencil and the layout is the same on every device.

Can I put lined paper between the slides of a PDF?

Yes. Choose Ruled lines as the paper style, either for a single gap or in the Every page dialog. Fill the rest of the sheet puts lines from the bottom of each slide to the bottom of its A4 sheet, which is the layout described in adding note lines under each slide.

Will the exported PDF open in GoodNotes and Notability?

Yes. It is a standard PDF. Long-page exports use the PDF user unit feature to allow very tall pages; current versions of GoodNotes, Notability, Preview, Acrobat and Chrome display them correctly.

Can I add space to a scanned textbook chapter?

Yes. Scanned pages are split at the point you click. Pick a gap between paragraphs or below a figure so the split does not cut through a line.

Does it handle a 200-slide deck?

Usually, yes. Very large PDFs are limited by your browser's memory rather than by PDFSpacer. If a huge deck struggles, split it into a few files first.

Can I come back and add more space later?

Yes. Your PDF and layout are saved in the browser, and Project > Download layout gives you a separate JSON file you can load again with the same PDF.

Can I add a whole blank page instead of a gap?

Yes. Click Every page in the toolbar and keep Full blank page selected to add a blank, ruled or dot-grid page after every page at once. See inserting a blank page after every page. Smaller spacers give you a gap under the paragraph instead.
