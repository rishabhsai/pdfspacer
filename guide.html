How to add space between questions in a PDF

A worksheet can have all the right questions and almost no room for your answers. PDFSpacer inserts writing space at a point you choose and moves the content below down. You can add room for working, written responses or notes inside an existing PDF.

The tool is free and runs in your browser. You do not need an account, and your PDF is processed on your device.

Insert blank space in four steps

  1. Open your document. Go to PDFSpacer, click Choose a PDF or Open PDF, and select a PDF from your device.
  2. Choose an insertion point. Select Add space in the toolbar. Click in a clear horizontal gap below a question, paragraph or diagram. Content below that point moves down.
  3. Set the amount and style of space. Select the spacer to edit its properties. Drag the resize handle to change its height. Choose Plain, Ruled, Dot Grid or Squared. Repeat wherever you need more room.
  4. Save the result. Click Export PDF. Choose Paginated A4 for standard printable pages or Single Long Page for a continuous document, then export.

Click between lines, rather than through a line of text. PDFSpacer splits the page at the height you select; it does not automatically detect where a question ends.

Add answer space to PDF worksheets and past papers

For a maths problem, insert a spacer just below the question and choose Squared for working or Plain for a sketch. For a written response, choose Ruled to add answer lines. Adjust each spacer to suit the expected length of the answer.

Example: a crowded two-question worksheet

Question 2 starts immediately after question 1. Click the gap between them, add a ruled spacer and increase its height. You now have lines for the first answer, with question 2 below the new space.

Repeat below question 2 if you need another answer area. Turn on A4 breaks to preview where the expanded worksheet may split, then check the exported PDF before printing.

You can move, duplicate or delete a selected spacer. Use the properties panel for Duplicate and Delete. Drag a spacer to move it or drag its bottom handle to resize it. Heights and pattern spacing are shown in millimetres on A4 paper.

Make room for notes and diagrams in a PDF

Insert a plain spacer after a section of lecture notes for a summary, or choose a dot grid for a diagram. The added area spans the page width, so it works best between blocks of content.

After export, open the PDF in your preferred annotation app to type or handwrite notes, or print it and write on paper. PDFSpacer creates the space; it does not add text answers or drawing tools.

Is this the same as adding margins or blank pages?

These solve different layout problems:

  • Space between questions: inserts an area inside the existing page and pushes later content down. Use PDFSpacer when the writing belongs directly below a question.
  • Wider margins: adds room around the edges of a page for side notes or binding. PDFSpacer does not change side margins.
  • Blank pages: adds a separate sheet between whole pages. PDFSpacer instead lets you choose an insertion point within the content.

Choose an export layout for printing or reading

Paginated A4 spreads the expanded document across A4 pages. Content flows across source-page boundaries. This is the layout to use when you want to print worksheets or past papers.

Single Long Page exports one continuous page for scrolling. It uses PDF page-size units to retain the full height of long documents. Choose A4 when you want a convenient print layout.

Both modes preserve the original PDF text and vector graphics; scanned text remains an image. Interactive forms, annotations and clickable links are not included. Use Export PDF → Preview to inspect the actual output before downloading.

Undo and keyboard shortcuts

Every completed edit can be undone, including a drag, resize, deletion or clearing all spaces. Use the Undo and Redo buttons, or Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z. With a spacer selected, press Delete to remove it or the up/down arrow keys to move it. Hold Shift for larger movements.

On a phone or tablet, tap to insert or select a space. Drag its body to move it and its bottom handle to resize it. Properties appear below the document. Use Fit to size the page to your screen.

Common questions

Can I add space to a scanned PDF?

Yes. A scanned page can be split and spaced in the same way. Choose a clear gap in the scan. PDFSpacer does not perform OCR or turn the scanned words into editable text.

Will this work on a two-column document?

A spacer crosses the full width of the page. Both columns move below the insertion point, so find a horizontal gap that is clear in both columns. It cannot add space to only one column.

Does the PDF leave my device?

The app processes your document locally in the browser and does not upload it to a server. The website loads its PDF libraries from CDNs. Your PDF and layout are saved in browser storage for session recovery; use Project → Clear Session to remove that saved session.

Can I come back to my layout later?

The app can restore a saved session in the same browser when browser storage is available. For a separate copy of your spacer layout, use Project → Download layout. Keep the original PDF too: the project JSON contains the layout, not the document.
